
# Appointment Policy

## Booking

Patients can request an appointment with an available doctor.

Appointments are subject to doctor schedules and available appointment slots.

Patients may request an appointment by providing:

- Patient name
- Doctor name or specialization
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Reason for visit

Existing patients must be identified using their registered patient information before an appointment is created.

## Doctor Availability

Doctor information and availability are maintained by the hospital appointment system.

Available doctors and appointment slots may change at any time.

An appointment is confirmed only after the selected slot has been successfully booked.

## Appointment Confirmation

Before booking, the patient should confirm:

- Doctor
- Date
- Time
- Reason for visit

The patient should receive confirmation after successful booking.

## Cancellation

Patients should cancel an appointment as early as possible if they cannot attend.

Cancellation is subject to the appointment's current status and hospital scheduling rules.

## Rescheduling

Patients who cannot attend their appointment may request a different available slot.

The new appointment time is subject to doctor availability.

## No-Show

A patient who does not attend a scheduled appointment without cancelling or rescheduling may be recorded as a no-show.

## Important

Doctor availability and appointment slots are live information and must be obtained from the appointment system.
