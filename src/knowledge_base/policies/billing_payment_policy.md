

# Billing and Payment Policy

## Payment Methods

The hospital may accept approved payment methods including:

- Cash
- Debit card
- Credit card
- UPI
- Other approved digital payment methods

Available payment methods may depend on the hospital service.

## Billing

Patients are responsible for charges applicable to the services they receive.

Hospital bills may include charges for:

- Consultation
- Diagnostic services
- Procedures
- Medicines
- Room or admission services
- Other hospital services

## Billing Information

Patients may request clarification of charges from the hospital billing department.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the type of payment and hospital service.

Refund requests should be reviewed by the hospital billing or finance department.

The hospital should not promise a refund until the applicable transaction and refund rules have been verified.

## Outstanding Payments

Patients may be required to settle outstanding amounts before completion of applicable hospital services.

## Billing Disputes

Patients who believe a bill is incorrect should contact the billing department for review.
