
# Contact and Escalation Policy

## General Assistance

Patients may contact hospital staff when they need assistance with:

- Appointments
- Registration
- Billing
- Insurance
- Medical records
- Other hospital services

## Human Assistance

A patient may request to speak with a hospital representative at any time.

Requests that cannot be resolved using available hospital information should be referred to the appropriate department.

## Appointment Issues

Appointment-related issues may require assistance when:

- A booking cannot be completed
- A patient disputes an appointment
- A required appointment slot is unavailable
- A patient needs help with a complex scheduling issue

## Billing Issues

Billing questions or disputes should be referred to the billing department.

## Insurance Issues

Insurance authorization, claim disputes, and reimbursement questions may require assistance from the insurance or billing department.

## Patient Information

Requests involving sensitive or patient-specific information should be handled only after appropriate verification.

## Emergency

Emergency situations should be handled immediately and should not be delayed by normal administrative procedures.

## Unavailable Information

If the required information is not available in the hospital knowledge base, the patient should be informed that additional assistance is required rather than being given an unverified answer.
