
# Emergency Policy

## Emergency Care

Emergency medical conditions should not be handled through a routine appointment.

Patients experiencing a serious or potentially life-threatening condition should seek immediate emergency medical care.

## Emergency Situations

Examples of situations that may require urgent medical attention include:

- Severe difficulty breathing
- Severe chest pain
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe bleeding
- Serious injury
- Sudden weakness or difficulty speaking
- Seizure
- Severe allergic reaction

These examples are not a diagnosis. The seriousness of a medical condition should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Emergency Assistance

Emergency services should be contacted when immediate medical assistance is required.

Patients should not delay emergency care while waiting for a routine appointment.

## Human Assistance

Patients may request assistance from hospital staff when they need help with emergency services or urgent hospital procedures.
