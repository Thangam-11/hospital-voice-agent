

# Insurance Policy

## Insurance Coverage

Insurance coverage depends on the patient's insurance policy and the insurer's approval.

Hospital admission or treatment does not automatically guarantee insurance coverage.

## Cashless Treatment

Cashless treatment may be available when:

- The patient's insurance policy is eligible
- The hospital is covered under the patient's policy/network
- Required authorization is obtained
- The treatment is covered by the policy

## Pre-Authorization

For eligible cashless treatment, the hospital may submit a pre-authorization request to the insurer or TPA.

The insurer reviews the request and determines the approved amount.

## Patient Responsibility

Patients may be responsible for expenses that are not covered by their insurance.

Examples may include:

- Non-covered services
- Policy exclusions
- Deductibles
- Co-payments
- Amounts exceeding approved limits

## Documents

Patients may be asked to provide:

- Insurance card
- Policy number
- Government-issued identification
- Medical documents
- Other documents requested by the insurer or TPA

## Reimbursement

When patients pay for treatment themselves, they may submit a reimbursement claim to their insurer according to the policy requirements.

The final reimbursement decision is made by the insurer or TPA.
