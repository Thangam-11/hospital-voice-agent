
# Patient Registration Policy

## Registration

Patients must have a patient record before hospital services requiring registration can be completed.

New patients may need to provide basic identification and contact information.

## Information Required

Registration information may include:

- Full name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Phone number
- Email address, when applicable

## Existing Patients

Existing patients should provide sufficient information to locate their patient record.

The hospital must avoid creating duplicate patient records.

## Patient Verification

Patient information must be verified before accessing or changing patient-specific information.

## Privacy

Patient registration information is confidential and must only be accessed by authorized personnel or systems.

## Updates

Patients should inform the hospital when important contact information changes.

Examples include:

- Phone number
- Email address
- Address
