

# Patient Privacy Policy

## Confidentiality

Patient information is confidential and should only be accessed by authorized personnel and systems.

## Patient Information

Patient information may include:

- Name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Phone number
- Email address
- Appointment information
- Reason for visit
- Other information maintained in the patient's hospital record

## Verification

Patient identity should be verified before providing patient-specific information.

## Information Disclosure

Patient information must not be disclosed to another person without appropriate authorization.

## Appointment Information

Information about a patient's appointments is considered patient-specific information.

The hospital should verify the patient's identity before providing appointment details.

## Data Security

Patient information must be protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or loss.

## Human Assistance

Requests involving sensitive patient information may be referred to authorized hospital staff.
