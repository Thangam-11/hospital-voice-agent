
# Visitor Policy

## General Visitors

Visitors must follow hospital visitor rules and instructions provided by hospital staff.

Visitors may be required to register at the hospital entrance or reception.

## Patient Areas

Access to patient-care areas may be restricted depending on:

- Patient condition
- Ward type
- Infection-control requirements
- Hospital operating procedures

## Critical Care Areas

ICU and other critical-care areas may have additional visitor restrictions.

Visitors must follow instructions from clinical staff.

## Children

Children visiting the hospital may be subject to age or area-specific restrictions.

## Visitor Conduct

Visitors must:

- Follow hospital staff instructions
- Maintain a quiet environment
- Respect patient privacy
- Follow infection-control requirements
- Avoid disturbing clinical care

## Restricted Access

Hospital staff may restrict or deny visitor access when required for patient safety, privacy, infection control, or clinical reasons.
